Ownership Verification

REBELLION II

To download, you must own Star Wars: Rebellion or Star Wars: Empire at War: Gold Pack. Sign in with Steam or GOG to verify ownership.

Sign in with Steam
Steam: check both privacy settings

In Steam, open Edit Profile > Privacy Settings. Set both My profile and Game details to Public for verification.

Check the account that owns the game. If the game is marked private, make it public for verification too.

Sign in with GOG
Why is Empire at War accepted?

Star Wars: Rebellion is no longer available for purchase through its former digital storefronts, leaving new players without the original—and preferred—ownership path. The installer therefore also accepts ownership of the commercially available Star Wars: Empire at War: Gold Pack.

This is only an alternative ownership check. Rebellion II does not copy, extract, install, or otherwise use any files, code, artwork, audio, or other assets from Empire at War.
