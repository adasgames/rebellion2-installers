# Launcher 1.0.7 candidate

## Fixes

- Fixed fresh installations downloading Content one file at a time after updating the game.
- Fixed outdated Content archives being accepted for newer game versions.
- Fixed missing or damaged Content being reported as up to date.
- Fixed unresponsive Update buttons and misleading download progress.
- Fixed interrupted updates and reinstalls restoring outdated files or version records.
- Improved recovery from locked files, full disks, and expired sign-ins.

## Updating

- After downloading this launcher update, close and reopen the launcher before updating the game.
